Set the topic mix

Jev files every tweet under one topic. Turn a topic off and it never makes your page. The sliders set roughly how much of the thesis each topic gets.

To add topics or change what counts under each one, go to Settings → Feed on your page.

How picky should it be?

This sets how good a tweet has to be to make your page. Later, the Filtered out tab shows what just missed.

Accounts to watch

Most stories come from your timeline panel, the accounts your preset's anchors follow. Add accounts here so they're always included.

Watchlist

People and outlets whose posts are always considered. Jev still judges each one. Type a handle and press Enter.

Tracked companies

Company accounts held to their own bar. A post counts when it clearly beats that account's usual likes, even if it isn't broadly viral.

You can edit the timeline panel and X searches in Settings → Sources on your page.

Connect your API keys

Your page runs on your own accounts, so nobody else's usage lands on your bill and yours doesn't land on theirs. Keys are encrypted on the server and never shown again.

Get it every day

Each morning, get the thesis, what's new today, and a link to your page. Set up any or none of these now, and add more later in Settings.
