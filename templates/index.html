Make this page yours

Every three hours this page reads what tech, AI, SF, and GTM Twitter is posting, keeps the tweets that matter, and writes a short thesis about the week. Take two minutes to set what it looks for and how you get it each day.

01FetchPosts, quotes, and retweets from a panel of accounts the scene follows, plus searches and a watchlist.
02JudgeJev scores each new tweet. Is it on topic? Is there real signal? Is it bait?
03WriteClaude writes the thesis and groups the best tweets into themes.
04DeliverThis page, plus a daily recap in Slack, Instinct, or Muse.

Steps marked Just you only change this device. Steps marked Team change the page for everyone, and nothing is saved until the last step.
